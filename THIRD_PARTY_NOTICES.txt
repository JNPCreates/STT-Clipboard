STT Clipboard third-party components

The application's MIT license covers this project's code. Bundled dependencies
retain their own licenses. The executable distribution includes a licenses folder
with notices collected from its installed packages and the Python runtime.

Major components include Python/Tcl/Tk, OpenAI Whisper and model weights,
PyTorch, NumPy, Numba/llvmlite, PyAudio/PortAudio, tiktoken, keyboard, pyperclip,
and the PyInstaller bootloader. PyTorch's license includes notices for its bundled
components. See each component's included notices for its terms.

Whisper: https://github.com/openai/whisper
PyTorch: https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch
PyAudio/PortAudio: https://people.csail.mit.edu/hubert/pyaudio/
PyInstaller: https://pyinstaller.org/

Keep the licenses folder and these notices with copies of the executable release.
